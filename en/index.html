Why Star Wish Pool

What kills habits isn't difficulty — it's silence. Here, every effort is seen, remembered, and rewarded.

Progress made visible

Your journey becomes a star map, and a streak flame keeps the count. Chests, meteors and eggs wait along the trail.

Effort that pays off

Stars are the record of your effort; redemption is your reward. Not indulgence — a way to make discipline sustainable.

Room to breathe

Missed a day? Make it up within 3 days. Sick or traveling? Take a rest day — your streak survives.

Twelve camps along the trail

From daily ratings to growth records, every corner is designed for keeping going. The trail on the left lights up as you scroll — just like the star map inside the app.

Daily Check-in Ratingsevery day · closed loop

Checking in is not the finish line — rating is. Score each task from 0 to 5 stars and see how well you really did, not just whether you did.

  • Do, rate, review, improve — a closed loop every day
  • Timely reminders; rate right after finishing, no backlog
  • Weekly bar charts — trends are more honest than feelings
  • After 8 pm, one tap settles everything for the day

Goal Managementbuild any habit

Turn "someday" into "today". Study, workout, read, rise early, sleep earlier — if you can name it, you can build it.

  • Custom categories, drag and drop to reorder
  • Effective dates and shared goals for the whole family
  • Difficulty hints nudge star levels up or down
  • Four ready-made template packs — tick to import

Sub-item Checklistsplit big goals small

"Finish this book" or "learn 500 words" turns into an endless daily task the moment it enters check-ins. Sub-item checklists split big goals into small steps — finish one, and one disappears.

  • Attach an optional checklist of up to 8 steps to any goal
  • Each step is one-shot: score it today and it's gone tomorrow
  • Every step is scored on its own — earn up to steps × stars in total
  • Finish everything and the goal "graduates" with a ✅ keepsake badge

Light the Star Trailthe core loop

Complete every task and one more node lights up on your trail. Passed spots reveal chests and star friends; the road ahead keeps its mysteries.

  • A streak flame guards the record you don't want to break
  • Trail-side chests pop open for 5 stars
  • Meet a zodiac friend every 7 days — all 12 in 84 days
  • 12 eggs combine into a hidden comet card

Expedition Log & Sharinghow far, at a glance

One line at the top of the map sums up the journey, and one tap turns it into a shareable night-sky card.

  • "N days · M stars · eggs · comet progress" in a single row
  • Tap any lit node to relive that day's tasks and photos
  • Share cards carry your route, zodiac dots and a download QR
  • Photo-only days get a 📷 mark — every moment stays

Wish Redemptionthe sweet side of grit

Earn stars by checking in, spend them on wishes. Persistence shouldn't be all bitter — it deserves rewards.

  • You define the wishes: a movie, bubble tea, a blind box, a trip
  • "N more stars to go" — progress in real time
  • Pending and redeemed tabs, history grouped by reward
  • Default reward templates to get started fast

Star Momentafter the stars are rated

Rating the stars shouldn't end the conversation. Once every task is scored, a "Star Moment" appears — the place for what you most want to say today.

  • Pops up once a day, never a nag; save it for later if you like
  • One-tap encouragements: "So focused today！", "Proud of you 🌞"
  • Or record a short voice message — mic permission only on first recording
  • Hand the phone to your child and hear what they want to say

Little One's Replyfeelings flow both ways

Your child opens the app to "You earned ⭐ ×N today" and your words — then it's their turn to tell you how the day felt.

  • Six moods to pick from: super happy, pretty good, confident, a bit hard…
  • Reply with a sentence or a voice note — or leave it all blank
  • Big buttons and no reading skills needed — every kid can join in
  • Both the encouragement and the reply go into the growth diary

Home Screen Widgetscheck in without opening

Put "what's left today" right on your home screen. One tap and today is settled — without interrupting what you were doing.

  • 2×2 display-only and 2×4 interactive sizes
  • One-tap full marks, done silently in the background
  • Auto-rotates every 30 minutes; tap to swap goals
  • Taps jump straight to the goal, scrolled and highlighted

Profiles for Everyoneone universe each

Big sister, little brother, mom, dad — separate star accounts, wishes and check-in records for everyone.

  • Switch profiles in one tap, emoji or photo avatars
  • Per-member goals, or goals shared across the family
  • Completion heatmap: three months at a glance
  • WebDAV cloud backup, last 5 versions kept

Growth Recordssee them grow

Log height, weight and eyesight with one tap, benchmarked against Chinese national health standards.

  • Height and weight in five bands, BMI computed for you
  • Left and right eyesight tracked separately
  • Multiple measurements with a full history to look back on
  • For everyday reference — not medical advice

Growth Diaryfeelings are worth keeping too

Photos, notes and Star Moments all live here. Stars record what got done today; the diary keeps why today mattered.

  • Star Moments as their own cards: ✨ header + date, parent in gold, child in teal
  • Replay voice messages anytime — real voices, kept as they were
  • Feeling-only days get kept too, photos or not
  • Single-file zip backup for switching devices — data, photos and voices included
